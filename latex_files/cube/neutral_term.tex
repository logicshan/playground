\vspace{10mm}
A neutral term is a variable, or an eliminator stuck on a neutral term. Basically a stack of eliminators applied to a variable.\\
~\\
These terms are called \textit{neutral}, and distinguished from other normal forms, because they have not reduced to a constructor, but they cannot reduce further (because the eliminators are stuck on a variable).
